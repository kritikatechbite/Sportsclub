Who we are

About Sportsclub

A sports publication for fans who want more than the final score – clear explanations, honest storytelling and a love of the game.

Our story

Built by fans, for fans

Sportsclub started with a simple idea: sports coverage should be easy to read, easy to trust and a pleasure to browse. We bring news, match updates and explainers for football, cricket, basketball, tennis, baseball and Formula 1 into one tidy home.

Whether you follow one team religiously or enjoy dipping into every sport, we aim to help you understand why things happen on the pitch, court or track – not just what happened.

This website is a design and development demo. Scores, fixtures, athletes and articles shown here are sample content.

Our mission is to make every sport easier to understand, more fun to follow and open to every kind of fan.
— The Sportsclub editorial promise
